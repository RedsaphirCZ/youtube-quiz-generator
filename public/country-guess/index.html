Notes from the geographer

Welcome, explorer.

Test your knowledge of 195 countries through their borders, flags, or a flag above a country border. Hide half of each clue horizontally, vertically or diagonally, use strips or checkerboard windows, or choose a different hiding style each country. You can also show the full clues.

Choose one of four answers. Each answer reveals the country and its capital, then you can uncover the full clues. Your expedition ends with a review of every stop. Best scores are saved separately for each hiding style, only in this browser. Nothing is sent to an account.

Flags: flag-icons / FlagCDN (MIT). Country outlines: Natural Earth (public domain). Country names and capitals: bundled REST Countries snapshot. The catalogue includes 193 UN member states, Palestine and Vatican City. Maps are learning illustrations, not authoritative boundary maps.
